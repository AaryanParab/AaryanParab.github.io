About Me

Game developer with a Postgraduate Degree in Game Design from Kingston University, UK, backed by a solid foundation in Information Technology and Software Development.

Day to day I work on a live Unity Android game hub — six real-time multiplayer titles sharing one Arabic-language app. That means Photon netcode, in-game voice and text chat, right-to-left localization, and a lot of gameplay UI, inside a shared codebase alongside a larger engineering team.

Before that I built 2D and 3D games in Unity and Unreal Engine at a game-a-week pace — a habit that keeps my prototyping fast, my scope honest, and my systems readable. I care about the part players actually feel: responsive input, clear feedback, and mechanics that stay fun after the novelty wears off.

> Ship it, play it, learn from it — then build the next one.
